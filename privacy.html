About the archive utility

Privacy policy

Beyond Our Kind VPS Archives is a personal utility operated by the owner of the Travisaggie04 GitHub account.

Information accessed

The app requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. It uses file contents and metadata, including names, identifiers, parent folders, sizes and integrity checksums, for archives it creates or files explicitly made available to it. It does not request full access to Google Drive, Gmail, contacts or calendars.

Use of information

Information is used to upload selected worker results, organize those archives, download them, verify integrity and restore useful results. Compact local records track archive identities and verification outcomes. An owner-authorized AI assistant may operate this workflow and inspect the selected development results as part of the owner's requested work.

The utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or use it to train generalized AI models. It does not collect unrelated files for analysis.

Storage and transfers

Archive files are stored in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's private server or devices as needed for active work and restoration. Google, the server hosting provider and any assistant service explicitly authorized by the owner process information needed to provide those services. Archive contents are not published by this information website.

Google authorization credentials are kept separately from archives in private configuration storage. Connections to Google use HTTPS; server administration uses SSH. Credentials are excluded from ordinary archive manifests and reports.

Retention, deletion and control

The owner controls which files are archived and how long they remain. Temporary restore copies may be removed after successful verification. The utility has no automatic policy of deleting authoritative source files or historical archives.

The owner can revoke the app's Google access through their Google Account's third-party connections controls. Revocation stops further authorized access but does not itself delete existing archives or local copies. Those can be removed through Google Drive and the owner's server or device file controls. Normal service retention and recovery rules may apply.

This information website

These static pages contain no application analytics, advertising, forms or tracking scripts. The website host may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site under its own privacy policy.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact the operator through the user-support email shown on Google's consent screen. Do not send credentials or private archives through public comments. Material changes to the use of Google data will be disclosed here, and additional consent will be requested where required before that changed use begins.

Effective 21 October 2026.
